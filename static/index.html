Rules & method

How a stock earns its score, and the rules that keep you in the game.

Read this first. No scanner knows what price will do next. SEBI's studies found roughly nine in ten individual F&O traders lose money. BlockRadar narrows 200+ stocks to a handful with a defined stop, so a wrong call costs a small, planned amount. It cannot turn a bad plan into a good one, and it is not investment advice.

Survival rules

  1. Risk 1% of capital per trade, or less. The calculator in every stock window sizes the lots for you. If one lot risks more than your limit, skip the stock.
  2. Always place the stop. The plan's stop is where the setup is wrong. Moving it further away is how small losses become account-ending ones.
  3. Cap the day. Stop trading after two losing trades or a 2% daily loss. Revenge trades are the biggest killer of accounts.
  4. Trade 1 to 3 setups, not 10. Take the highest scores that are In zone or Approaching with reward to risk of 1:2 or better.
  5. Do not chase. If a card says Extended or Target hit, the entry has passed. Wait for the next one.
  6. Treat 09:15 to 09:55 as a watchlist. Signals appear from the first candle but carry an Early tag, because there is very little price history yet. They firm up after about 40 minutes. Results days and big news also break patterns.
  7. Paper trade it for two weeks first. Log each signal and what happened. Trust the numbers you gathered, not a screenshot.
  8. Mind the margin. A single F&O lot is often worth several lakh rupees. Leverage magnifies losses exactly as it magnifies gains.

How the score (0 to 99) is built

25Trend

Price versus VWAP and the day's open, higher highs and lows (or lower), VWAP slope, day change.

25Order block quality

Impulse size in ATR, volume on the impulse, how fresh and untested the zone is.

20Liquidity

A recent stop-hunt (sweep) that reclaimed, and real liquidity levels to run toward.

15Flow

Volume versus normal for this time, strength versus Nifty and sector, order book imbalance.

15Entry readiness

How close price is to the zone and the reward-to-risk of the plan.

Points are removed when price keeps crossing VWAP (choppy), when the first target has already been hit, or when the stop is broken.

Terms

  • Order block (OB): the last opposite candle before a strong, structure-breaking move. Large players often have unfilled orders there, so price tends to react when it returns.
  • Liquidity: prices where many stop orders sit, such as the previous day's high or low, equal highs and equal lows. Price is often pulled to these before reversing.
  • Sweep: a wick through a liquidity level that closes back inside. A stop-hunt.
  • VWAP: volume-weighted average price for the day. Above it favours buyers, below it favours sellers.
  • ATR: average 5-minute candle range, used to size zones, stops and distances.
  • Reward:risk (R:R): distance to Target 2 divided by distance to the stop.

Data and limits

  • Live mode reads Upstox 5-minute candles, daily history and quotes through a local server. The token is read-only and stays on your computer.
  • Volume buy/sell split is estimated from where each candle closed within its range. It is not exchange tick data.
  • Rankings refresh after every 5-minute candle closes. Prices and states update every few seconds in between.
  • Before 09:55 IST (fewer than 8 candles) stocks are ranked from the opening candle, previous-day levels, volume and strength versus Nifty. If no order block exists yet, the first candle is used as an opening block (marked OR on the chart). Only the strongest 25 per side are shown.
  • Scores are rule-based and have not been back-tested here. Treat them as a shortlist, then use judgement.
BlockRadar is an educational analysis tool. It does not place orders and is not investment advice. Derivatives trading carries substantial risk of loss.
